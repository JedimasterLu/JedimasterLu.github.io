  - 'This project systematically investigated the composition-parameter-structure-property relations of combinatorial super-latticelike thin films of GeSbSn(Se) and GeSbSeTe using ion beam deposition and various high-throughput characterization methods. Base on high-throughput characterizations, we constructed the multi-parameter coupled structure-perperty relationships of the ternary and quaternary phase change material systems. The project was supervised by Prof. <a href="https://smse.sjtu.edu.cn/people/detail/274">Hong Wang</a>. My graduation thesis on GeSbSeTe system won the Best Bachelor’s Thesis (top 1%) in 2025. These works have been publish in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matdes.2026.116922">Materials & Horizon</a> and <a href="https://doi.org/10.20517%2Fmicrostructures.2024.100">Microsctructures</a>.'
